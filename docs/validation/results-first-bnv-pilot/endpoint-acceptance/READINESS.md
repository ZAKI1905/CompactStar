## Meaning of the nonlinear result

The local witness already includes finite exponentials, the exact chemical
rank-one inverse, structural finite-flow remainder and tangent variation
(`../surface-transfer/METHOD.md:1`). Those nonlinear **surface-transfer** terms
are now charged conditionally over the whole required remapping domain. This
closes C-CUT; it does not bound the nonlinear time evolution of temperature and
chemical imbalance. The latter still requires original-t=0 references or a
verified error-transport enclosure and the stated event/roundoff terms
(`../../PHASE6_CAMPAIGN_ACCURACY_PREDECLARATION.md:67`).

Trajectory-time criteria cannot truthfully be passed before a trajectory runs.
They should remain mandatory acceptance tests for a separately authorized
experiment. However, even the pre-execution gates are not all complete here:
the native matched assembly/snapshot/runner gates above are still open.
Consequently the requested success condition for an evolution authorization
request is not met. This delivery issues no launch request and no executable
trajectory submission script.
